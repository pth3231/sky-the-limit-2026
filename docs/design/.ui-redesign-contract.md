# Draft workspace surface brief

Mode: Operate. Scope: the League of Legends draft workspace and its champion picker. The job is to support coaches through a live pick/ban draft with trustworthy estimates and historical context; preserve current draft rules, accessible keyboard behavior, and factual uncertainty.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A champion decision is clearest when the live pick/ban board and coaching evidence share one field; this refuses both the broadcast ticker-first page and decorative game-client chrome.

OWN-WORLD: User-pinned League champion select, grounded in `docs/design/champ_picker.png`: near-black and deep burgundy surfaces, restrained muted-gold frames, blue/red team cues, compact portrait cells, familiar select states, and no licensed League fonts or artwork. Preserve readable names and clear text labels in every state.

STORY: Coaches read each team's picks and bans, see which action is live, compare rated candidates with explicit explanations, and keep the projected series chance and guidance in view. Unrated champions stay neutral; unavailable roles and historical players stay unknown rather than fabricated.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact setup/status bar spans the top. Cloud9 and opponent columns flank the centered, internally scrollable champion grid; search and role controls remain fixed. A compact coaching strip sits directly below the grid, with detailed insights and series history independently scrollable. At 1280×720 and 1920×1080, the document itself does not scroll; narrow screens stack the same regions with essential actions and guidance visible.

FORM: The League-inspired reference and integrated-insights layout are pinned by the user and override assigned seed candidate 3 (seed `101c0e79`); its description was not emitted, so it is not guessed. Challengers were weighed on audience identification and product clarity. The timetable is competitive for keeping the active action tied to true draft order; the acetate manual is competitive for fixed, visible controls; the Crouwel specimen is competitive for a consistent portrait-cell grid. The exposure record, cassette card, and mascot catalog were declined; retain their useful disciplines only: distinct truthful score semantics, readable draft chronology, and verified portrait/name pairing without fabricated personality or ratings.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
